# FinSolve Technologies — Customer Support FAQ

## Account Opening
Opening a FinSolve account takes about 5 minutes through our mobile app. You'll need a valid government-issued ID, proof of address, and your Social Security Number or equivalent tax ID. Accounts are typically approved within 24 hours, though some require manual review which can take up to 3 business days.

## Business Hours
Our customer support line is open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM. We're closed on Sundays and major US holidays. The mobile app and website are available 24/7 for account management, transfers, and balance checks.

## Fees and Charges
FinSolve checking accounts have no monthly maintenance fee. Wire transfers cost $15 for domestic and $35 for international transfers. ATM withdrawals are free at any of our 40,000 partner ATMs; out-of-network ATMs incur a $2.50 fee plus whatever the ATM operator charges. There are no overdraft fees — transactions that would overdraw your account are simply declined.

## Lost or Stolen Card
If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately from the mobile app under Card Settings, or call our 24/7 emergency line. A replacement card ships within 2 business days for standard delivery, or you can pay $25 for next-day delivery. Any fraudulent transactions made before you report the card are fully reimbursed under our Zero Liability policy.

## International Transfers
FinSolve supports international wire transfers to over 80 countries. Transfers typically arrive within 1-3 business days. Exchange rates are updated in real time and shown before you confirm the transfer, with no hidden markup beyond the standard $35 fee.

## Security and Two-Factor Authentication
All accounts require two-factor authentication, either via SMS code or an authenticator app. We recommend the authenticator app since SMS can be vulnerable to SIM-swapping attacks. FinSolve will never call or email you asking for your password or full card number — treat any such request as fraud.

## Closing an Account
You can close your account anytime from the app under Settings > Close Account, provided your balance is zero and there are no pending transactions. If you have a balance, request a transfer to an external account first. Account closure is immediate and cannot be undone; you'd need to reapply to reopen.

## Interest Rates on Savings
FinSolve's high-yield savings account currently earns 4.25% APY, compounded daily and paid monthly. Rates are variable and tied to the Federal Funds Rate, so they can change; we notify customers by email at least 30 days before any rate decrease.
